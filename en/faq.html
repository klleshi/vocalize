Guides and frequently asked questions

How to use Vocalize.

Step-by-step guides for the main features and answers to the most common questions about credits, subscription and account.

Guides

All features start from the Home screen, below the text box.

Listen to a text
  1. In the Home tab, type or paste the text in the box.
  2. Choose Language and Voice. If you don't, Vocalize detects the language of the text and sets it for you.
  3. Tap Play. Stop ends the reading.

Long texts are read in blocks: listening starts right away while the rest is prepared.

Import a PDF or TXT file
  1. Tap File below the text box.
  2. Pick a PDF or TXT file from your phone.
  3. The text appears in the box: tap Play.

Clear empties the box.

Import a web page
  1. Tap Web below the text box.
  2. Paste the page link (it must start with https://).
  3. Vocalize extracts the article text and puts it in the box.
Adjust speed, pitch and volume

Tap Speed, Pitch or Volume below the text box and move the slider. Default restores the initial values.

Save the audio as MP3 and share it
  1. After the reading, tap Save and name the file.
  2. You'll find it in the Saved File tab, where you can play it again, rename it, export it or share it with other apps.
Create an audiobook with chapters
  1. Save as MP3 the texts you want to use as chapters.
  2. Open the Audiobook tab and tap +.
  3. Enter the title, pick a cover if you like and add chapters from your saved files.
  4. Save: the audiobook resumes where you left off.

The free plan lets you create one audiobook; Premium has no limit.

Use the sleep timer

During playback, open the player by tapping the bar at the bottom, then tap Timer and choose when to stop the audio.

Frequently asked questions

Credits and Premium

How do credits work?

Each reading uses credits based on the characters in the text and the type of voice. Your balance is in the Premium tab.

Every month, on the day you started using the app, your balance goes back to your plan's maximum: 200,000 credits on the free plan, 800,000 with Premium. Unused credits don't roll over. There is also a daily limit to keep usage fair.

How many credits do the different voices use?

Standard and WaveNet voices use 1 credit per character, Neural2 voices 4 credits and Studio voices (Chirp HD) 8 credits. On the free plan, Neural2 and Studio voices use a separate allowance of 25,000 trial credits.

How much does Premium cost?

€4.99 per month or €49.99 per year (prices for Italy, VAT included; in other countries the store shows the price in your local currency). Premium includes 800,000 credits per month, all voices, unlimited audiobooks and no ads.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by the stores. On Android: Google Play → profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. On iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Premium stays active until the end of the period you already paid for.

How do I restore my subscription on a new phone?

Install Vocalize, sign in with the same Google or Apple account you used for the subscription (Premium tab → Save your credits) and tap Restore Purchases.

Account and data

Do I need an account?

No. On first launch Vocalize creates an anonymous account and you can use the app right away. If you link a Google or Apple account (Premium tab → Save your credits), you keep your plan and credits when you change phone.

The Premium page shows "Connection error"

The app can't reach the service. Check your internet connection and tap Retry: as soon as you're back online, your balance appears by itself.

Is the text I read stored?

No. The text is sent to the speech service only to create the audio and is not stored. Saved audio files and audiobooks stay only on your phone. Details in the privacy policy.

How do I delete my account and data?

Write to klleshi99@gmail.com from the address linked to your account, or tell us how you sign in to the app. Your account and data are deleted within 30 days: details are on the account deletion page. Saved audio files are removed by uninstalling the app.

Ready to listen to your texts?
