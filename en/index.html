Frequently asked questions

The most common questions. Step-by-step guides and all other answers are on the dedicated page.

How do credits work?

Each reading uses credits based on the number of characters and the type of voice: Standard and WaveNet voices cost less, Neural2 and Studio voices cost more. Your balance is shown in the app's Premium tab.

Every month, on the day you started using the app, your balance goes back to your plan's maximum. There is also a daily limit to keep usage fair.

How do I restore my subscription on a new phone?

Install Vocalize, sign in with the same Google or Apple account you used for the subscription (Premium tab → "Save your credits") and tap "Restore Purchases".

How do I delete my account and data?

Write to klleshi99@gmail.com from the address linked to your account, or tell us how you sign in to the app. Your account and data are deleted within 30 days. Details are on the account deletion page.

Saved audio files and audiobooks are stored only on your phone: uninstalling the app removes them.
